About the podcast

A seat at the table with the people behind the businesses.

Founders Table by RAG Holdings brings founders, entrepreneurs and business leaders together for meaningful conversations. From starting a business to navigating challenges and exploring opportunities in Dubai, each episode shares experiences and insights from the people behind the businesses.

Meet the host

Deeraj Rao

Group Marketing Head, RAG Holdings

Deeraj leads marketing at RAG Holdings across the UAE, Qatar, Singapore and India, building the team that handles ads, content, PR and the brand’s digital presence.

His career started behind the microphone. Over six years at 93.5 Red FM in Bengaluru he produced the breakfast and evening drive-time shows, picking up recognition at the New York Festivals Radio Awards and the Indian Radio Forum Awards. He went on to run IPL and ICC T20 World Cup campaigns at ITW Consulting, and brand integrations for Disney Star shows at The Walt Disney Company.

Off the clock, he’s the Community Head of RCB Fan Army UAE.

What is Founders Table?

Founders Table is the signature podcast of RAG Holdings. Each episode is a long-form conversation with a founder or business leader about their journey, the challenges they faced and the ideas shaping business today.

Who hosts the podcast?

The podcast is hosted by Rassal Ahmed, Founder & CEO of RAG Holdings, together with Deeraj Rao.

Where is Founders Table recorded?

Every episode is recorded in our dedicated studio at RAG Biz HQ on Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai.

Where can I watch the episodes?

Full episodes are on our YouTube channel, and short clips and highlights are posted on Instagram. Each episode runs about 35–50 minutes.

How can I be a guest on the podcast?

Fill in the Be a guest form with your details and the story you’d like to share. Our team reviews every application and reaches out to shortlisted founders.

Who can apply to be a guest?

We welcome founders and business leaders of any size and from any industry — what matters most is a story worth sharing.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Applying to be a guest and suggesting a founder are both free.

Can I suggest someone else to be on the show?

Yes! Use the Suggest a guest form below. Share the founder’s name and LinkedIn profile, tell us why they belong at the table, and add any questions you’d like us to ask them.
